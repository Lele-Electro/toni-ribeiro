This policy explains what personal information {{ siteName }} ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use, store and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to our private lift club and referral business services.

Information we collect

When you create an account with email and password

  • Your first name and, if you choose to give it, your surname.
  • Your email address.
  • Your password, which we never store in readable form. We keep only a one-way bcrypt hash of it.

When you sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares the following with us, and only after you agree on Google's consent screen:

  • Your name (given name and family name).
  • Your email address, and whether Google has verified it.
  • Your Google profile picture URL.
  • A unique Google account identifier, which lets us recognise you the next time you sign in.

We request only the basic openid, email and profile permissions. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive files or any other Google data, and we never receive your Google password.

When you use the scheduling form

  • The dates and times you choose, the service each one is for, and your time zone.
  • Any note you add, such as a pick-up point.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To save the dates and times you choose and show them back to you.
  • To arrange your lift or consultation and contact you about it.
  • To keep the service secure, for example to detect misuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use information received from Google for any purpose other than signing you in and showing your name and picture in your account.

Google user data

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Access: we only receive the name, email address, profile picture and account identifier listed above.
  • Use: we use them only to create and sign in to your account and to personalise your account pages.
  • Storage: we store them with your account in our database, protected as described below.
  • Sharing: we do not share them with anyone except the hosting providers that run this service for us, and never for advertising.
  • Deletion: they are deleted when you delete your account.

You can also remove our access at any time from your Google Account connections page.

How we store and protect your information

Your account and saved dates are stored in a MongoDB database run by our hosting provider. Information is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. When you sign in we issue a short-lived token (valid for one hour) that your browser keeps in local storage so you stay signed in while you use the site. Signing out removes it.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who host our website, API and database on our behalf, under agreements that require them to protect it, or where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved dates for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, your saved dates and any information received from Google straight away.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: sign in, open My Schedule and choose "Delete my account". This permanently removes your account and everything stored with it.
  • Remove a saved date: use "Remove" next to it on My Schedule.
  • Access or correct your information: email us at {{ contactEmail }}.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws (such as South Africa's POPIA or the EU GDPR) may give you further rights, including to object to processing or to complain to a regulator.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in. If you use Sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies to run the sign-in process, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell signed-in users about significant changes.
