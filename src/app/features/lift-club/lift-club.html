How a lift is arranged

  1. Pick a date and time

    Choose when you need to be picked up and tell us where in the note.

  2. We match you to a route

    We place you with a driver already travelling your way at that time.

  3. Ride and share the cost

    Meet at the pick-up point and settle your share of the trip with the driver.

Common questions

Who can join the lift club?

Anyone with an account can request a seat. Regular members are invited into fixed route groups once we know your usual times.

How much does a lift cost?

The cost of fuel and tolls on a route is shared between the people travelling. We confirm your share before your first trip.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. You can remove a selection from your schedule at any time, and pick a new date and time.
